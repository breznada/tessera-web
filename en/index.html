About us

About us

We are consultants in sensitive data security. We work in DevSecOps and application and infrastructure security — from design through to operation.

We focus on the places where security meets day-to-day operation: credentials, certificates and sensitive data, whose management in modern infrastructure has outgrown what manual procedures can cover.

Finding unsecured credentials across systems

02

Secure storage and auditing of sensitive credentials across the organisation

We centralise the management of credentials, keys and certificates. Access is issued dynamically and time-limited, rotated automatically, and every issue is recorded in an audit trail. This covers the NIS2, DORA and ISO 27001 requirements on access control and auditability.

NIS2DORAISO 27001
03

PKI automation — internal and external certificates

We deploy an internal certificate authority for mutually authenticated service-to-service communication (mTLS) and automate both issuance and renewal, including integration with public certificate authorities. With the lifetime of public TLS certificates shortening to as little as 47 days, this is now a precondition for running at all. It relates to the NIS2 and DORA requirements on encrypted communication.

mTLSinternal CAexternal CANIS2DORA
04

Data transformation

Masking, pseudonymisation and tokenisation of sensitive data — typically for production data in test and development environments, for analytics and reporting, or to reduce the number of systems handling payment data. It narrows PCI DSS 4.0 audit scope and satisfies the GDPR requirement for pseudonymisation. Format-preserving encryption lets existing applications stay unchanged.
